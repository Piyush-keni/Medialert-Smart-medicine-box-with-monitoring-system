KiCad schematic design

Maps the signal path from the ESP32's GPIO pins through the ULN2003 driver to the 28BYJ-48 stepper, alongside the I2C bus feeding the LCD and the IR receiver / buzzer interrupt lines used for dose confirmation and alerts.

KiCad PCB layout

Two-layer board with isolated power traces for the stepper driver, ground-plane pour for noise reduction around the IR sensor, and labeled footprints for the ESP32 dev module, LCD header, and screw terminals.
